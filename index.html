YouTube Otomasyon

YouTube Otomasyon is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It adds captions to short comedy videos created by its owner and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Data the app accesses

The app asks for two Google permissions on the owner's own account:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel name and the status of uploaded videos.

How data is used and stored

The sign-in token and all videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server. It does not collect data about viewers or anyone else, and it does not sell or share data. Data obtained through Google APIs is sent only to YouTube, in order to upload the video.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access from the computer.

Third-party terms

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
